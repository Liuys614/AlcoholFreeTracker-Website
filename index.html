Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Where is my tracker data stored?

Your profile, check-ins, drink levels, times, and notes are stored locally on your device. The app does not upload your personal logs to any external server.

Do I need an account?

No. Alcohol Free Tracker works immediately without registration, login, or an internet connection.

Can I track moderation ("drink less") as well as complete sobriety?

Yes. Daily check-ins let you log an alcohol-free day or record a light, moderate, or heavy drink level without resetting your logged reflection streak.

How do I back up or transfer my data?

Open Settings, choose Export, and save the portable JSON file to your files or iCloud Drive. To restore it on a new phone, choose Import.

Is this medical treatment?

No. The app is a self-directed tracking tool, not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, counseling, or emergency withdrawal support.
